GFLOW STUDIO PORTABLE - WINDOWS 64-BIT

1. Bam chuot phai tep ZIP -> Extract All / Giai nen tat ca.
2. Mo thu muc GFlow-Studio-Portable -> bam dup MO-UNG-DUNG.bat.
3. Giao dien mo trong trinh duyet. Giu cua so dong lenh mo.
4. Nhan Dang nhap Google -> dang nhap trong cua so Chrome -> Kiem tra phien.

KHONG chay setup-windows.bat cua ban cu.
KHONG can cai Python, pip, uv hay Visual C++ Build Tools.
Python 3.12 va thu vien Windows da duoc dong goi san, gom lz4 da bien dich.
Khong su dung Python 3.15 tren may ban. Khong thay doi Python he thong.

Can Windows 10/11 64-bit va Google Chrome da cai tren may.
Tao anh/video can Internet va tai khoan co quyen Google Flow.
Video su dung credit tai khoan. Khong kem tai khoan hay credit.

Phien Google luu trong data/profiles. Anh/video luu trong app/out.
De xoa: dong ung dung, xoa toan bo thu muc GFlow-Studio-Portable.

Ung dung tu chon cong trong de tranh xung dot voi ban dang mo.
Neu trinh duyet khong tu mo, xem so cong trong cua so dong lenh
va nhap http://127.0.0.1:SO_CONG (thay SO_CONG bang so hien tren man hinh).
