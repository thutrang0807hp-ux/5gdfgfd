GFLOW STUDIO - HUONG DAN WINDOWS

1. Giai nen toan bo tep ZIP (Extract All). Khong chay ngay trong ZIP.
2. Cai Python 3.12 tu https://www.python.org/downloads/windows/
   Chon "Add python.exe to PATH". Can co Google Chrome tren may.
3. Mo thu muc 5gdfgfd, bam dup setup-windows.bat.
   Buoc nay can Internet, tai thu vien va Chromium; chi can lam lan dau.
4. Khi bao thanh cong, bam dup start.bat trong cung thu muc.
5. Giao dien mo trong trinh duyet. Neu khong tu mo, truy cap:
   http://127.0.0.1:8765
6. Nhan Dang nhap Google, dang nhap trong cua so Chrome.
   Nhan Kiem tra phien, sau do tao anh/video.

Giu nguyen hai thu muc 5gdfgfd va gflow-cli canh nhau.
Giu cua so dong lenh mo khi su dung. Nhan Ctrl+C de dung.
Video dung credit Google Flow cua ban.

Day la ban ma nguon kem tep cai/chay, khong phai tep EXE.
Giao dien va API da duoc kiem thu tren Linux voi Chromium.
Chua kiem thu tren Windows hoac tao anh/video thanh cong voi tai khoan Google.
Can tai khoan Google co quyen dung Flow; khong kem tai khoan hay credit.
